# MEStrong
## What is MEStrong?
MEStrong is a tracker for your gym workouts which is being developed by three students, Martin Stättner, Elyesa Güngür and Selim Chatti, from the TGM.

## Why MEStrong?
Our product is one of the easiests trackers on the market. While you often have to create your workouts manually we thought of doing it the other way around. We let the app decide your workout, based on your preferences and goals.

## Release Date
We are currently hard working on the project. You can already sign up, if you don't already have an account, or log in right now <a href='mestrong.fit'>HERE</a>, play around and get your next personal training partner to know. The first launch with full functionality is planned in Januray 2027.
